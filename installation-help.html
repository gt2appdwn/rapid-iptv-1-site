Rapid IPTV Device Setup Guides

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Begin by checking your confirmation email. It includes the server address, username, and password for your account. Our support team is available on WhatsApp to guide you step by step until your device streams smoothly.

Quick start in 3 steps

Every device follows the same simple flow. Pick your device below for the exact steps.

1
Check your emailYour confirmation email has the server URL, username and password.
2
Install a player appChoose your device below and install one of the listed apps.
3
Add your loginChoose Xtream Codes API, enter your details and start watching.

Amazon Fire TV Stick, Fire TV 4K and Cube

Watch on Firestick or Fire TV by installing an IPTV player app, then entering your server details from your confirmation email.

IPTV Smarters ProTiviMateDownloader
1

Download the Downloader app

Open your Firestick main screen, go to Find > Search, type Downloader, select the orange Downloader icon in Amazon Appstore, then choose Get.

2

Allow Downloader to install apps

Go to Settings > My Fire TV > Developer Options. Select Install Unknown Apps and turn Downloader to ON.

Do not see Developer Options? Go to Settings > My Fire TV > About, highlight your Fire TV name and press the centre Select button 7 times until you see "You are now a developer".
3

Get an IPTV player

Look for IPTV Smarters Pro or TiviMate in the Appstore, or install one using Downloader.

4

Sign in using Xtream Codes

Open the IPTV app, pick Xtream Codes API, enter your server URL, username, and password from your email, then press Add to load your channels and movies.

Smart TVs: Samsung Tizen, LG webOS and Hisense

Access Rapid IPTV directly on Samsung or LG Smart TVs with an IPTV app, using the server link and login sent by email.

IBO PlayerSmart IPTVNanomid
1

Install the IPTV player app

On your Samsung Smart Hub or LG Content Store, search for IBO Player or Smart IPTV, then select Install.

2

Find your Device MAC and Key

Open the app on your television. The home screen shows your TV's Device MAC Address (for example b4:0e:22:xx:xx:xx) and the Device Key.

3

Access the login portal

On a computer or phone, visit https://iboplayer.com/device/login. Enter your Device MAC and Key, then click Login.

4

Include your Xtream playlist

Click Add XC Playlist, enter a playlist name, then fill in your server URL, username, and password from your email. Select Save and restart the app to watch.

Android TV, Google TV, Nvidia Shield and Android boxes

Set up on Android TV, box, or phone by downloading a supported IPTV app and entering the server and account details supplied after signup.

TiviMate IPTV PlayerIPTV Smarters Pro
1

Pick a player from Google Play

Search for TiviMate IPTV Player or IPTV Smarters Pro in Google Play Store and install either app.

2

Select Xtream Codes API

Open the app, tap Add Playlist, then choose Xtream Codes API.

3

Enter your IPTV details

Fill in the server URL, username, and password from your email, then tap Next.

4

Load your channels and guides

The app will download your live channels, TV guide (EPG), movies, and series. Pick any channel to begin watching.

Apple iOS (iPhone and iPad) and Apple TV

Use your iPhone, iPad, or Apple TV by installing an IPTV player and entering the server address, username, and password from your Rapid IPTV email.

IPTV Smarters ProGSE Smart IPTVIBO Player
1

Get the player app from App Store

Open the App Store on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple TV 4K. Search for Smarters Player Lite, IBO Player, or GSE Smart IPTV and tap Get.

2

Launch and agree to terms

Start the app on your Apple device, then accept the user agreement.

3

Log in with Xtream Codes API

Choose Login with Xtream Codes API. Enter any name, your username, password and server URL.

4

Start watching or cast to TV

Tap Add User to watch on the device or cast live to any AirPlay-compatible TV.

Windows PC, laptop and Mac

Stream from Windows or Mac computers with any compatible IPTV player, adding your credentials from the Rapid IPTV confirmation message.

IPTV Smarters Pro DesktopVLC Media Player
1

Access the IPTV Smarters Pro application

Download IPTV Smarters Pro for Windows (.exe) or Mac (.dmg). Open it, pick Xtream Codes API, enter your username, password, and server URL, then tap Add User.

2

Stream with VLC Media Player

Open VLC, press Ctrl + N on Windows or Cmd + N on Mac to open Network Stream. Paste your M3U link from the email and hit Play.

MAG boxes (250, 254, 322, 420, 524) and Formuler

MAG box users can watch by configuring the portal address with the server link and credentials we provide after registration.

Built-in portalMyTVOnline
1

Find the MAC address on box

Look for the MAC address starting with 00:1A:79 on the label under your box or in Settings > System Settings > Device Info.

2

Send MAC address to support

Contact us on WhatsApp or by email with your MAC address so we can enable your box.

3

Configure the portal settings

Go to Settings > System Settings > Servers > Portals on your box. Set Portal 1 Name to Rapid IPTV and Portal 1 URL to the link from your confirmation email.

4

Restart box to load channels

Save your settings, exit, then select Reboot Device. Your TV guide will appear after the box restarts.

Roku sticks and Roku TV

While Roku does not support direct IPTV apps, some users use third-party solutions. Setup details are available after you contact our support team.

Screen mirroringApple AirPlayWeb Video Caster
1

Cast from your phone or tablet

Open IPTV Smarters on iPhone, iPad, or Android. Tap the Cast or AirPlay icon and pick your Roku. Your channel will show on TV.

2

Open the Web Video Caster application

Install Web Video Caster on your phone and Roku. Paste your M3U link from the email, select Roku, and tap play to stream.

Roku doesn’t support IPTV apps directly, so casting is the way to watch.

Troubleshooting

Check your inbox and your junk or spam folder. If the email is not there, message us and we will resend it.
Type the username, password and server URL again exactly as written in the email, with no spaces at the start or end.
Restart the app and your device, then check that your internet connection works in another app.
Message us on WhatsApp or email us. Support is available 24/7 and we help until your device is streaming.

Message us and we will help you until your device is streaming. Support is available 24/7 by email and WhatsApp.
